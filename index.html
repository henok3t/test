A climb in six rungsThe Ladder of Abba Moses

Egypt, the fourth century. Moses is a robber chief, strong and feared. Before the end he will be one of the gentlest of the desert fathers.

Move with WASD or the arrow keys · Space to jump · Shift to kneel · G to give · drag to look around.Left thumb moves · right thumb jumps, kneels and gives · drag anywhere else to look around. Works upright or sideways.

Holy Abba Moses, pray to God for us.

St. Moses the Ethiopian was a robber who became a monk of Scetis in the Egyptian desert. Around the year 405, when raiders came, he refused to take up the sword or to flee, and was killed with seven of his brothers.

The sayings in this game are from the Sayings of the Desert Fathers, the Lausiac History and Holy Scripture.
